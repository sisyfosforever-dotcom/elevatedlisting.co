Refund Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Free preview first

You see a watermarked preview at no cost before paying anything (Standard $249, Elevated $499, Maximus on request). Payment happens only after you choose a package.

When you can get a refund

  • Before final delivery – a full refund if you ask before the clean, final film is delivered.
  • Late delivery – a full refund if delivery is more than 7 days past the promised 5–7 days after go-ahead and payment.
  • Not what you approved – a refund if the delivered film materially differs from the approved preview.
  • After delivery – other fixes are handled through your revision rounds.

How refunds work

Crypto payments are final on-chain and can’t be reversed by a bank, so all refunds are handled directly by Luis. Email luis@elevatedlisting.co with your order details; approved refunds are sent back to the paying wallet, in the same stablecoin, within 14 days.
