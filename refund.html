Refund Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

You see the film first

You see the finished film, with a watermark, before paying anything. If you love it, you say yes and pay $249, once. Payment is by crypto (USDC on Base or USDT on Arbitrum) or Western Union only. I invoice you by email after you say yes. Card payment is not available yet.

When you can get a refund

  • Before delivery – a full refund if you ask before I send the clean film.
  • Late delivery – a full refund if I don’t deliver within 48 hours of your payment reaching me. The clock starts when the crypto arrives in my wallet or the Western Union transfer is ready for me to collect, and I confirm that by email.
  • Not what you said yes to – a full refund if the delivered film is clearly different from the film you saw.
  • After delivery – other changes are handled through your one round of tweaks.

How refunds work

Crypto and Western Union payments can’t be reversed by a bank, so all refunds are handled directly by Luis. Email luis@elevatedlisting.co with your order details; approved refunds are sent back the same way you paid within 14 days: crypto goes back in the same coin to the wallet you paid from, and Western Union goes back by Western Union to the name on your transfer.
