Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Overview

Elevated Listing (the trading name of Mats Ludvig Lindberg, known as Luis Lindberg, a private individual; not a company and not registered as a business) respects your privacy. We collect as little as we need to reply to you and to deliver the work. We do not sell personal data.

Data controller: Mats Ludvig Lindberg. Contact: luis@elevatedlisting.co.

What we collect

Contact form (FormSubmit)

  • Name, email, listing URL (optional), message, optional “listing as a song” note, and reason checkboxes.
  • Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards them to our inbox.

Payments

  • You can pay by PayPal to the PayPal Business account “Elevated Listing” as Goods and Services, by crypto (USDC on Base or USDT on Arbitrum), or by Western Union (steps at elevatedlisting.co/pay). I invoice you by email after you say yes.
  • If you pay by PayPal, you pay PayPal and PayPal pays Luis (the PayPal Business account “Elevated Listing”). PayPal processes your payment as an independent third-party payment processor under its own privacy statement. I never see or store your card or bank details; I receive the payment record PayPal shows me (such as your name, PayPal email, amount, date and note).
  • PayPal records, wallet addresses, Western Union transfer details and invoices are kept for accounting and, if needed, to send a refund back the same way you paid.
  • We receive order and fulfillment info so we can deliver the film and song.

Basic technical data

  • Standard hosting/logs (e.g. IP, browser) may be processed by GitHub Pages to serve and secure the site.

How we use it

To answer inquiries, produce and deliver your film and song, handle refunds or support, and keep the site secure. We don’t use your data for unrelated advertising lists.

Sharing

We share data only with services needed to run the business — mainly GitHub Pages (hosting), FormSubmit (contact form), and, if you choose PayPal, PayPal as a third-party payment processor. Crypto payments are recorded on the public blockchain; Western Union handles its transfers under its own terms. We don’t sell personal data.

Sales emails & opting out

I sometimes email property owners a short, personal note about a film. Every such email includes a way out: reply STOP, or email luis@elevatedlisting.co with subject STOP, and I add your address to a do-not-contact list and won’t email you again. I keep only what is needed to honour that request.

Retention & your choices

We keep contact and project records as long as needed for the work and reasonable bookkeeping. To access, correct, or delete personal data we hold, email luis@elevatedlisting.co.
